# Foreword
**Normal**, **Intermediate**, **Advanced**, and **Expert** are intended for 6 players.

Some of these missions may not be balanced for 6 players despite being labeled as Expert;

this is in large part due to these missions being playtested with 20 players and Diffmod

in mind.

<br>

**Master:** The highest difficulty for 6 players. Click Expert missions

are of similar difficulty. Any missions beyond this difficulty are not intended to be

beatable with a team size of 6. DIFFMOD can be expected to handle 3x HP except for:

Coastal Cashout (Coastrock) and GRIEF (Underground).

<br>

**Hypermaster:** Prefixed in the votemenu with hypmas_mission_name, missions of this

difficulty are intended to be beaten with anywhere between 12 and 16 casual players.

20-player teams can consider these missions to be on par with 6-player Intermediate or

Advanced missions depending on the difficulty of the specific Hypermaster. 6-player

lobbies are not intended to beat these missions. DIFFMOD can be used to apply 2x HP

for a more challenging experience.

<br>

**Grandmaster:** Prefixed in the votemenu with gndmas_mission_name, missions labeled with

Grandmaster difficulty are intended for 20 players with difficulty on par with 6-player

Expert missions. DIFFMOD should not be used on these missions.

<br>

**Ultimate:** Prefixed in the votemenu with ult_mission_name, these are the hardest missions

on the server. 20-player casual teams should not expect to consistently beat these missions.
